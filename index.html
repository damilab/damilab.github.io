About Us

Welcome to Data Analysis & Machine Intelligence Lab at Dongguk University. Our lab aims at designing practical data science and machine learning systems that are ethically fair, privacy and security assured, algorithmically stable, and interpretable to humans.

Our current research spans three directions: Trustworthy AI, the safety and security of large language models, AI agents, and robots; Physical AI, robots that see, plan, and act safely in the real world; and LLM Data & Agents, data generation and LLM agents for manufacturing, blockchain, and finance.
